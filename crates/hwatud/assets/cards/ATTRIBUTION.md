# Hanafuda card art

All 48 SVGs by [Louie Mantia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hanafuda_July_Tane.svg),
from Wikimedia Commons, licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Files are named `<deal-index>-<month>-<type>.svg`. Deal order is
row-major over the deck laid out as 12 month columns x 4 rows
(row 1 = each month's highest card), so deal 12 (the 13th card)
is January's second-row card.
